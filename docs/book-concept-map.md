# Book concept map

This repository demonstrates engineering patterns inspired by *Being Human in the Age of Artificial Intelligence*. It does not encode the book's full philosophical or Christian argument.

| Book question | Lab mechanism | Boundary of the analogy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can the human genuinely supervise? | Approval exposes action, target, risk, evidence and scope | Software cannot certify understanding |
| What may the machine do? | Roles, scopes, deterministic policy, separate approver | Demo actor header is not authentication |
| Who answers for consequences? | Correlated audit events and responsibility trace | Attribution is not moral responsibility |
| What happens to capability after delegation? | Four-mode `DelegationAdvisor` considers maintenance and atrophy | Advice is illustrative and contextual |
| What is the action for? | Proposal reason and explicit policy | A policy does not establish a worthy ultimate purpose |
| Is the human in the loop? | Approval changes executable authority state | A click alone is insufficient without competence and practical control |

The modes are Retain, Augment, Delegate, and Periodically Reclaim. They are not a moral ranking. The right boundary depends on the person, practice, purpose, responsibilities and conditions.
