# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, patient and constructive. Assume good faith while discussing ideas, give specific technical feedback, and welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels. Harassment, threats, discrimination and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable.

When a public repository is established, report conduct concerns to its maintainers through the repository's available moderation channels. Maintainers may remove harmful contributions or restrict participation. No personal contact address is designated here.
